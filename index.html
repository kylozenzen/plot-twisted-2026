The Plot Twisted formula.

Reel 01

Bad summaries.
Very good movies.

The clues tell the truth. Technically. They just explain beloved movies like a deeply confused coworker caught you near the microwave.

Reel 02

The theater is
the interface.

The seats become your keyboard. Tap on mobile or use the real keys on desktop without leaving the movie-night illusion.

Reel 03

Close enough
still counts.

Fuzzy matching handles small typos, missing articles, and sequel chaos. This is trivia, not a spelling bee with popcorn.

Reel 04

Every showing
ends with a stub.

Your ticket stub shows the score, the critic rating, and whether you set a house record. Share it for extremely reasonable bragging.

Keep the theater

Install Plot Twisted. Skip the app-store side quest.

Add the game to your home screen or desktop and launch straight into the theater. It stays a web app, updates automatically, and does not ask for an account.

Play without installing
Checking what your browser supports…

iPhone or iPad

Safari
Home screen
  1. Open Plot Twisted in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then confirm.
The marquee icon lands next to your other apps and opens straight into the game.

Android

Chrome
Install app
  1. Tap the install button on this page when it is available.
  2. Or open Chrome's menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm the installation.
The exact menu wording can vary slightly by browser and Android version.

Mac, Windows, or Chromebook

Chrome or Edge
Desktop app
  1. Click the install icon in the address bar when it appears.
  2. Or open the browser menu and choose the option to install Plot Twisted.
  3. Confirm, then launch it from your applications or dock.
No separate download package is required; the browser handles the installation.
